📖 The Rounds – Game Guide

The Rounds is a bar-themed word-grouping puzzle. Sixteen words appear on the board — your job is to find four groups of four words that share a hidden connection.

How to Play

  1. Study the 16 words displayed on the board.
  2. Select four words you believe share a common theme or connection.
  3. Press Submit Round to check your group.
  4. Correct groups are removed from the board and colour-coded by difficulty.
  5. Find all four groups to complete the puzzle!

Colour Difficulty Guide

  • 🟡 Yellow — Easiest group. Usually the most obvious connection.
  • 🟢 Green — Medium difficulty.
  • 🔵 Blue — Harder — look for sneaky double meanings.
  • 🟣 Purple — Hardest — tricky wordplay or bar-culture knowledge.
